❯ Write exactly 120 words about the history of lighthouses.

⏺ Lighthouses trace back to antiquity, when fire beacons on hilltops or towers guided sailors toward harbors. The most
  famous ancient example, the Pharos of Alexandria, built around 280 BCE, stood over 100 meters tall and was considered
  one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Through the Middle Ages, coastal towers using open flames or candles
  marked dangerous shores, though inconsistently. The eighteenth century brought major innovation: Smeaton's Eddystone
  Lighthouse pioneered interlocking masonry construction, while Argand lamps and parabolic reflectors sharpened beams.
  Fresnel lenses, invented in 1822, revolutionized visibility by concentrating light efficiently across long distances.
  Electrification followed in the nineteenth century, and by the twentieth, automation replaced resident keepers. Today,
  GPS has diminished lighthouses' navigational necessity, yet many remain preserved as historic landmarks, symbols of
  maritime heritage, and cherished cultural icons worldwide.
