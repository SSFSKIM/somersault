Cell — `cluster-expand-cells.sh` / `run_cluster_expand_cell`:
  1. Launches fake-host.mjs, gets its SHORT id.
  2. Pushes `thinkcluster` to the fake host's OWN stdin BEFORE `ccx attach` is ever launched — the
     FIRST-EVER push, no warmup sentinel. This deliberately exercises T-ATTACH's merged
     replay-on-follow contract (a frame pushed before the first `follow` is buffered and replayed,
     marked replay:true, to the first follower) rather than the already-following path
     linkopen-cells.sh's cells use.
  3. Launches `ccx attach <short>` (the real built binary) and waits for "mode on".
  4. Asserts the collapsed summary row ("Read 2 files") is present, and the thinking phrase
     ("zebra quartz reasoning") is NOT yet visible (premise check, avoids a vacuous cell).
  5. Locates the collapsed row's column via linkopen-col-of.py, sends a plain-click SGR
     press+release (button 0), and waits for "zebra quartz reasoning" to appear.
  6. Asserts a `∴` gutter line is present, and that in capture LINE ORDER:
     "Read(/work/alpha.txt)" < "zebra quartz reasoning" < "Read(/work/beta.txt)" — i.e. the
     absorbed thinking body renders strictly BETWEEN the two Read member rows.
  7. Clicks again to collapse. FINDING (see below): the click must NOT land on the `Read(...)`
     member header's own path argument, because that argument carries an OSC-8 hyperlink
     (T-LINKOPEN) and a plain click landing inside a link range resolves to "no target" rather
     than a fold toggle (FullscreenViewport.tsx's `clickTargetAt`: the link check runs before the
     fold-anchor check and swallows the click). The cell instead clicks the absorbed thinking
     body's own text ("First I skim..."), which carries no link and is tagged with the same fold
     anchor as every other row the expansion drew.
  8. Asserts the summary row ("Read 2 files") is back and the thinking phrase is gone.

Diagnostic note (not a defect — a real click-routing property, verified live via a throwaway
debug script before the cell was finalized): a plain click on `⏺ Read(/work/alpha.txt)`'s header
row did nothing (capture unchanged) because the click landed inside the path's hyperlink range;
the identical click one row down, on the thinking body's plain text, collapsed the cluster
immediately. The shipped cell's second click targets the thinking-body text for exactly this
reason, documented inline in the script.
